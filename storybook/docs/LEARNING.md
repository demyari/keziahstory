# Learning design

The rule: learning is a side effect of exploring. Nothing is compulsory, nothing is graded,
there are no failure screens, and every learning moment is part of something a character
needs.

## Alphabet

- **Coverage:** all 26 letters, each with a letter name, a gentle sound hint ("S says sss"),
  1–5 picture words (apple, ant, airplane, avocado; bear, balloon, butterfly...), and notes
  where English is irregular (I, U, X).
- **Where letters appear:**
  - **Story events:** letter bubbles (pop the S or s), signposts (which sign has M?), magic
    letters to trace.
  - **Character names:** S for Sparkle, H for Hoot...
  - **Place signs:** the first letter is highlighted; tap it to hear the letter and its sound.
  - **Drop caps:** tap one to hear its letter.
  - **The Discovery Book:** each letter page lists the words met in stories.
- **Both cases:** upper and lower case both count, and both are shown everywhere.
- **Variety:** the example word changes from story to story.
- **Order:** new letters arrive in a phonics-friendly order (s a t i p n c k e h r m d g o u l
  f b j z w v y x q), two or three per story.
- **Revisiting:** a letter found only with a hint or a friend's help counts as "growing" and
  comes back in the next story in a new place. Letters found independently twice become
  "familiar" and are rotated in less often.
- **Mistakes:** a wrong tap names the letter it was ("That one is M"). After two misses the
  right one glows. The Help button lets a friend show how. All three outcomes are recorded
  softly.

## Counting

- **One tap per object:** the object changes (wakes, lights up, lands in the basket), a number
  badge stays on it, a rising note plays, and the number is spoken. Double taps are ignored.
  Finishing says the total ("Five mushrooms!").
- **Kinds of counting:**
  - tapping each thing: waking things, singing stones, footprints;
  - carrying or dragging one at a time: lanterns, feeding a friend, filling a basket to
    exactly N, which teaches stopping at the number;
  - counting as things fall: shaking a tree;
  - matching quantities: picking the basket with N, where a wrong basket is counted aloud
    for comparison.
- **Numerals:** shown on the baskets only once counting is confident (level 2+, or after three
  confident counts), keeping quantity and numeral recognition separate at first.
- **Progression:**
  - 1–5 at the start;
  - 3–10 after five confident counts near the top of the range;
  - 6–20 after five more, with large quantities laid out in rows to help one-to-one counting.
  - If four counts in a row need help, the level steps back quietly.
- **Discovery Book:** numbers 1–20 with ten-frames. Tap each object to count along, and see
  which things were counted in stories.

## Privacy of the learning profile

The profile is a local, soft estimate: counts of independent, hinted and helped moments per
letter and number. It is never sent anywhere. The parent area explains it in plain words
("getting to know", "seems familiar") with no percentages or grades.
